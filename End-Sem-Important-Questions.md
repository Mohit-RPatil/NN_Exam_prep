# End-Sem Important Questions

This file contains a compiled list of important questions for the End-Semester Examination, categorized by unit. The questions are sourced from previous years' papers, assignments, and important question files.

---

## Unit 1: Introduction to Neural Networks

- **Frequently Asked:**
    - Explain the different types of activation functions used in neural networks. Discuss their advantages and disadvantages.
    - Differentiate between supervised and unsupervised learning.
    - Compare and contrast Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) with Biological Neural Networks (BNNs).
    - Explain the significance of weights and biases in a neural network.
    - Differentiate between feedforward and recurrent neural networks.

- **Other Important Questions:**
    - What are the key characteristics of an Artificial Neural Network (ANN)?
    - What is the role of the bias input in a neural network?
    - How do weights and biases affect the learning process in a neural network, and what methods are used to update them during training?

## Unit 2: Linear Networks and Multi-Layer Perceptrons

- **Frequently Asked:**
    - Explain the ADALINE model and its learning algorithm.
    - Describe the backpropagation algorithm. What are its merits and demerits?
    - Explain the Least-Mean-Square (LMS) algorithm.
    - What is the Wiener-Hopf equation and its significance?

- **Other Important Questions:**
    - Differentiate between linear regression and sequential regression.
    - Explain the method of steepest descent and its use in training Adaline.
    - How is function approximation and interpolation achieved using Multi-Layer Perceptrons?
    - Write short notes on MEDALINE.
    - How does ADALINE differ from linear regression in terms of learning?

## Unit 3: Self-Organizing Systems

- **Frequently Asked:**
    - Explain the architecture and learning algorithm of Kohonen's Self-Organizing Map (SOM).
    - What is topological mapping in the context of SOM?
    - Explain the Generalized Hebbian Algorithm (GHA).
    - What is Principal Component Analysis (PCA) and the Karhunen-Loeve transform?

- **Other Important Questions:**
    - What are local learning laws? Provide examples.
    - Compare and contrast Oja's rule and Sanger's rule.
    - What is pattern clustering and what are some techniques used in neural networks?

## Unit 5: Radial Basis Function Networks and Kernel Methods

- **Frequently Asked:**
    - Explain the architecture and functioning of a Radial Basis Function (RBF) Network.
    - How do Support Vector Machines (SVMs) handle non-linearly separable data using kernel functions?
    - What is regularization theory and why is it important?
